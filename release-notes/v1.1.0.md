**Hint Plus 1.1.0** adds a built-in **speed test**. Update under **More → Updates**.

## New: speed test
- Measures **download, upload, ping and jitter** through your gateway, using Cloudflare's public speed test servers (the ones behind speed.cloudflare.com).
- Every result is saved with your gateway's **signal at that moment** (band, RSRP, SINR) and your phone's Wi-Fi link speed, so you can compare spots in the house and bands, and see when your phone's Wi-Fi is the bottleneck.
- Find it under **More → Speed test**, on the **Home** screen, or as a launcher shortcut (long-press the app icon).
- **Open Speedtest by Ookla** opens Ookla's app if you have it, or speedtest.net.
- A test uses up to about 400 MB (usually less). On mobile data the app asks first, since the test wouldn't measure your gateway.
- The last 50 results stay on your phone. Cloudflare sees your IP address during a test, like any website; see [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/The412Banner/HINTControl/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## Install
`HintPlus-v1.1.0-<build>.apk`, signed with the public AOSP test key (v1 + v2 + v3). Android 8.0+. Check the download against the `.sha256` file.
