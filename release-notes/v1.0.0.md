First stable release of **Hint Plus**: an Android app to view and control T-Mobile Home Internet gateways.

Hint Plus is an Android-only rewrite of [HINT Control](https://github.com/zacharee/HINTControl) by **Zachary Wander (zacharee)**, used under the MIT License. The gateway communication, data formats and many safety warnings come from his work.

## Tested on a real gateway
Arcadyan **TMO-G5AR**, firmware 1.00.06: found automatically, login, status, 5G signal, live history and connected devices. Other models supported by HINT Control (Arcadyan KVD21/G4AR, Sagemcom 5688W, Sercomm G4SE/G5SE, Nokia 5G21) are covered by automated tests but not yet confirmed on hardware. Reports are welcome.

## Highlights
- Home dashboard with a 0–100 signal score, ratings for RSRP, SINR, RSRQ and RSSI, and bands with their frequency
- Placement mode: a tone and vibration that rise as you move the gateway to a better spot
- Signal history up to 30 days with band-change tracking and CSV export
- Devices: your own names, new-device badge, private-MAC detection
- Wi-Fi: edit networks, hide/show the network name, QR sharing, radio settings, review every change before saving
- Reboot with progress, scheduled reboots, admin login change (TMI gateways)
- Alerts for offline, restart, weak signal and new devices; event log
- Widget, Quick Settings tile, shortcuts, automation broadcasts
- Several gateways, demo gateway, Material You and dark themes, layouts for phones, tablets and landscape handhelds
- In-app updates from this repository, checked against the published SHA-256

## Fixed compared with HINT Control 1.17.3
- G5AR / G5SE: Wi-Fi devices didn't show (found by **cmarker84** and **fitzmorrispr** in [HINT Control #123](https://github.com/zacharee/HINTControl/issues/123))
- Editing the 6 GHz channel or width overwrote the 5 GHz settings
- Nokia 5G21: SIM always shown as inactive; login used saved instead of typed credentials
- The gateway's reported location was never read
- The gateway password was stored unencrypted

## Updating from a test build
If you have a v1.0.0 test build (pre1–pre5), the app offers this release under **More → Updates**; your gateways, history and settings stay.

## Install
`HintPlus-v1.0.0-<build>.apk`, signed with the public AOSP test key (v1 + v2 + v3). Android 8.0+. Check the download against the `.sha256` file.
